**Aggregate indicative incremental cost at maturity:** Rs 150–250 billion per year (excluding the Rs 568 billion arrears and the one-off protection package), against an estimated annual conflict loss of Rs 400–800 billion — a ratio of roughly 1:2 to 1:4 in favour of investment (Chapter 15).

**Priority ordering if resources are constrained (the "first six commitments"):** #2 (pay the arrears), #1 (legislate the transfer), #3–#4 (protect and compensate the police), #15 (staff the courts), #24 (elect the councils), #27 (education), #31 (publish the annual report). These are sequential in credibility terms: each makes the next politically survivable.
