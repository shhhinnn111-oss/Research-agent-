# BIBLIOGRAPHY

**Note on citation practice.** In-text citations follow Chicago author–date. Sources are listed by type. News and database sources are cited by outlet and date, consistent with the study's monitoring record (data cut-off 4 October 2026; access date 4 October 2026 for continuously updated sources). The study's mandate forbids invented sources; accordingly, a small number of short-form citations drawn from the project's working monitoring log that could not be matched to a full bibliographic record within the verification window are listed in Part VIII in the exact form recorded, with no embellishment. Militant-group media claims are attributed to the claiming outlet, never to a neutral authority. Contested figures (official, independent, and militant) are attributed rather than reconciled throughout the text.
